Film review detective

The Lion King (1994) · Read the review, then identify what each paragraph does and how the reviewer builds an opinion.

Lesson objective

Recognise the features of a film review.

Success criteria

1 · Label the paragraphs

Drag each job to its paragraph. On a tablet, tap a job, then tap its paragraph box. You may change your choices.

2 · Highlight the review’s features

Choose a colour, then tap the points in the review. Tap a marked point again to clear it. A point can contain more than one detail; choose its main job.

Selected: positive evaluation

Facts are green; recommendations are magenta. Red marks details that give too much away.

3 · Circle film review words

Circle film terms, genre terms and evaluative words or phrases. Tap again to undo.
